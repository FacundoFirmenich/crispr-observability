# Same-fragment validation gate

The target populations are the three original HEK3 Figure 2b_10 runs and the nine primary CFTR Figure 4a runs. All archived CFTR input streams used by P1 and the three historically declared duplicate deposits are searched for another observation of those records. The parent study catalogs are screened in full for paired runs, matching sample or experiment identifiers, and matching Figure 2b_10 or Figure 4a aliases.

Admit a molecular comparison only if the raw data contain two distinct target-covering observations with a documented link to the same original physical unit. For paired-end data require a common flowcell, instrument/run, lane, tile and x/y with different read ends, or a formally paired archive spot with both biological reads. Preserve the distinction between an amplified insert and a pre-PCR genomic molecule. For other technologies require authenticated pre-amplification molecular identity or a documented physical measurement of the same molecule. Identical bases, same donor, same target, sample indexes, accession ordinals from different runs, and reanalysis of the same FASTQ are insufficient. A common cluster and same end are the same sequencing observation; compare both sequence and quality to identify redundant copies and retain any discrepancies. A study-level search, either positive or negative, has the archive snapshot as its scope.

The HEK3 human-label join requires the original row-level annotations and authenticated read identifiers or a uniquely reversible row representation. The published marginal counts do not bind any individual raw record. To assess the latter requirement, enumerate exact raw-sequence multiplicities in SRR10285861. Treat unresolved joins as missing. Count exact original read IDs that coincide with the frozen three-extractor CFTR disagreement family and the finite-record witness panels, without inferring molecule accuracy from their coverage. If there are no eligible second observations, report accuracy as not estimable and execute the identity, provenance and redundancy audits in full.
