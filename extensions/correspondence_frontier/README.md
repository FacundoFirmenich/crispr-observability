## Results and jurisdictions

The selected 89 PE discrepancies partition exactly: 44 respond to orientation,
45 to removal of the dedicated PE gap incentive. This is a fixed partial
factorial, not a complete causal analysis of every upstream option or a claim
of software error. PE incentive zero agrees with reverse competing-reference
projection in all 89.

All 398 CFTR and 66 HEK3 read–reference cases pass complete optimal-path
enumeration and orientation-set transport. Single selected codings change in
142 and 36 cases respectively. Native-local CFTR status is invariant in 306
cases versus 256 invariant strings. HEK3 programmed-tract status is invariant
in 48 cases versus 30 invariant strings. These are conditional computational
certificates, not validated accuracy gains.

The integer objective uses match 5, mismatch −4, N mismatch −2, N–N −1, gap
opening −20 and extension −2, with the inspected upstream terminal convention.
It is distinct from the upstream returned percentage identity. Completeness
covers this fixed objective only. Forward extraction includes left-boundary
insertions and excludes right-boundary insertions; reverse transport must
exchange that ownership. An unresolved optimum cannot be silently discarded.

CFTR joint targets obey exact-programmed ⇒ native-local. HEK3 programmed and
unedited local tracts are mutually exclusive. Joint polygons are sharp convex
hulls of per-record candidate-domain completions. They are neither confidence
regions nor all finite attainable counts, and they do not impose a single
globally shared unknown observer across every read.

HEK3 rules were fixed before raw outcomes were inspected, but study selection
used known published summaries. This is retrospective fixed-rule transport,
not blinding, prospective registration or independent-laboratory validation.
No matched HEK3 negative control or treated-control effect is invented.

Published human-review marginals on 383 selected records identify only possible
agreement ranges: [272,382]/383 for CRISPR-A and [275,377]/383 for CRISPResso2.
The inspected author materials did not supply authenticated linked labels.
The ranges do not establish actual accuracy or a ranking, and do not refute
the source authors' individual-label benchmark. Mathematical transport
completions are not asserted to be observed biological records.
