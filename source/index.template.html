Interpretation

Where prime editing enters the argument

In prime editing, a Cas9 nickase makes a single-strand nick, and a reverse transcriptase copies information from the RNA template. A resulting product can differ in length. The first experiment asks how that length change can move a downstream anchor relative to the observation boundary. The parameter is a coordinate displacement in the readout model. The lab does not predict editing, cut positions or efficiency. Anzalone et al., Nature (2019)
