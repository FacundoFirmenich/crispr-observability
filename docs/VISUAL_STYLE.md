# Visual conventions

Use a white or pearl background and a clear, restrained hierarchy. Assign series
and comparison groups colours from blue, sky blue, indigo, violet, lilac, purple,
pink, fuchsia, salmon, subdued orange, and compatible ochre. Pale silver and cool
light grey may support separators and secondary structure. Use conventional black
or white text where appropriate. Data marks and status encodings use the approved
palette, with deep blue or purple for dark accents.

Avoid yellow, green, red, graphic black, and brown. Keep saturation vivid and
controlled. Reserve mild neon accents for a clear visual purpose. Use text labels,
line styles, or shapes alongside colour when they improve legibility.

Use vector artwork for publication where possible. Keep denominators, categories,
units, uncertainty, and the distinction between missing and zero visible.
Preserve the original source figure and link each redesign to its source data.
