# Three research lines, with their actual integration states

[Castellano](RESEARCH_LINES.es.md)

The infrastructure is used and adapted across three research lines in the
authors' programme. This is documented reuse, not three independent external
adoptions or three interchangeable biological validations.

| Line | Scientific question | Implemented use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CRISPR Observability** | What sequence and event can a finite editing readout support? | Native browser laboratory, CFTR leading case, evidence graphs, replay, CLI/API/MCP | Original-fragment biological editing accuracy remains a separate validation target |
| **Biogen / BioGen S1 — local splicing observability** | Does a required combination of splice junctions occur in the same record of the chosen mate? | v0.5 junction engine applied to retained biological alignment records; same-record and uniqueness endpoints kept separate | Retained-input replay; not a fresh raw-read alignment campaign or evidence imported into the CRISPR result |
| **ChronoWitness — mathematical biology and local temporal dynamics** | How do temporally and spatially local observations support a declared comparison? | Separate temporal adapter with five cases and seven operations, executable check/run and provenance | Shared browser/MCP integration is pending; the temporal schema is not silently coerced into a molecular schema |

The Biogen integration comes from engine commit
`d6a12e4dae968250d6791fdee281ea15c1cfd25b`. The ChronoWitness status is documented
in its adapter README and substantive integration record, reviewed on 2026-11-12.
Their unpublished scientific inputs are not redistributed here. New adopters
should use a new namespace, an explicit coordinate/units contract and a minimal
shareable case. Claims of independent use will require attributable evidence.
