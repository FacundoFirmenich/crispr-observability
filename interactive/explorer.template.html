Finite-record arithmetic

Each record is an accepted event, accepted non-event or unknown.

E + NE + U = N
p ∈ [E/N, (E + U)/N]

The attainable fractions are discrete: (E + k)/N for integer k from 0 to U. The displayed interval is their hull.

Δ ∈ [Eₜ/Nₜ − (E꜀ + U꜀)/N꜀,
(Eₜ + Uₜ)/Nₜ − E꜀/N꜀]
    These are identification hulls conditional on correct accepted labels, not confidence intervals.
