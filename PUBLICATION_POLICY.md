# Public distribution boundary

Code, numerical results, data provenance, scientific evidence archives and the claim/evidence graph may be public.

The manuscript and formal supplementary document, including their LaTeX and editable manuscript sources, must remain outside this public repository and its public releases. They may be added only after the author explicitly confirms acceptance by bioRxiv and authorizes their inclusion. A scientific software release or a Zenodo DOI does not establish that acceptance.

The author-review documents are maintained separately. This policy does not change the attribution or licensing of original public source studies, and it does not disclose their private author-review package.
