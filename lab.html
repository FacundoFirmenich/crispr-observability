How to use this lab

    What to look for

    Move the read boundary

    Shift the downstream anchor or change how far the read extends. Coordinates are zero-based and half-open.

    Compare candidate spans

    Choose two extraction rules or set either interval yourself. The observed sequence is held fixed.

    Inspect modeled atoms

    Crop the 42-pair model or isolate a strand. Click an atom to read its model-space coordinates in Å.

    Inspect your structure

    Filter chains and pick two atoms in the scene to test a distance threshold.

    Audit a local alignment

    Select a record, trace its CIGAR, and inspect exact coverage for a reference interval.

    Where prime editing enters the argument

    In prime editing, a Cas9 nickase makes a single-strand nick, and a reverse transcriptase copies information from the RNA template. A resulting product can differ in length. The first experiment asks how that length change can move a downstream anchor relative to the observation boundary. The parameter is a coordinate displacement in the readout model. The lab does not predict editing, cut positions or efficiency. Anzalone et al., Nature (2019)
